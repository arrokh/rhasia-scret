The models are scaled and positioned for the landing scene. Their display surfaces are overlaid with the app's interactive, synthetic preview UI. The assets do not imply Apple endorsement.

The iPhone and MacBook meshes are licensed visual stand-ins, not exact meshes of the 2026 product revisions. The tablet is modeled in Three.js with machined-aluminum, glass, camera, control, and port details. The iPhone's unused display/filler texture references are removed from its GLB because its screen is replaced by the live app component. MacBook texture maps are served as separate PNGs, allowing browser caching and keeping them out of the model's initial GLB payload.
