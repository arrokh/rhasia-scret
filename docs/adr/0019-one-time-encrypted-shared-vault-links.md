# One-time encrypted shared-vault links

An owner can invite an exact email recipient immediately by creating a one-time Secure Share Link and delivering it through a secure out-of-band channel. The owner’s client encrypts the vault-key handoff package; after the recipient signs up or signs in and completes cryptographic enrollment, the link lets their client activate membership without returning a usable Vault Encryption Key to the server or requiring the owner to return.
